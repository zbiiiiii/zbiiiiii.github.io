Calculator Lock: Photo Vault
Privacy Policy

SIYA Studio provides Calculator Lock: Photo Vault, a calculator with a PIN-protected private library and notes. This policy explains what stays on your device, how advertising services process information, and how to remove your data.

Your private content

Photos, videos, album names, favorites, notes, and preferences are stored locally in Android private app storage. The app does not upload your vault content or PIN to SIYA Studio or include that content in advertising requests. There is no online account or cloud synchronization service. Media is not separately encrypted; private notes use a separate AES-256-GCM encryption layer with a device-bound key. Your PIN is stored as a salted, iterated hash.

Optional biometric unlock uses Android authentication. The app receives an authentication result, not fingerprint images or biometric templates. Optional face-down quick hide processes accelerometer readings locally; it does not retain or transmit motion readings.

Advertising and consent

The app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. The initial closed-testing release uses Google's test advertising units. Advertising and consent services may process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information to provide ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. We do not operate a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

Google may act as an independent data controller for some processing. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google advertising information. Where required, Google's consent tools present available privacy choices. You can reopen available choices from Settings → Advertising privacy choices. Availability depends on your region and Google's requirements. You can also manage advertising preferences through Android's privacy settings and My Ad Center.

Import, permissions, and sharing

You choose files through Android's system picker. Import creates a private copy and leaves the original in your gallery. Delete any unwanted original yourself. The app does not request broad photo-library access on modern Android versions. Older Android versions may request storage permission for an export you initiate. Internet access is used by advertising and consent services. If you export or share a file, the destination app or storage provider handles that exported copy under its own rules.

Retention and deletion

Media moved to the recycle bin is retained according to your selected retention setting until automatic removal or permanent deletion. You can permanently delete items from the bin, delete notes, or use Settings to erase the active library. A decoy library is separate; remove its contents separately. Clearing Android app data or uninstalling removes local libraries, preferences, and the device-bound note key. Export important files first: no cloud backup, account recovery, or forgotten-PIN recovery service is provided. Android cloud backup and device transfer of private app data are disabled.

SIYA Studio cannot retrieve or delete vault files remotely. Information Google holds is subject to Google's retention and deletion controls. Contact us if you need help understanding these controls.

Security

The app uses Android private storage, PIN verification, automatic locking, and screenshot protection in its normal release. These protections do not guarantee security on a compromised or rooted device. Keep your device and code secure.

Children

Calculator Lock is a general utility intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. Contact us with any privacy concern involving a child.
