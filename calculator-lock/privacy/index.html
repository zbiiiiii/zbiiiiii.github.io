Calculator Lock: Photo Vault
Privacy Policy

SIYA Studio provides Calculator Lock: Photo Vault, a calculator with a PIN-protected private library and notes. This policy explains what stays on your device, how advertising services process information, and how to remove your data.

Your private content

Photos, videos, album names, favorites, notes, and preferences are stored locally in Android private app storage. The app does not upload your vault content, PIN, or recovery key to SIYA Studio or include that content in advertising requests. There is no online account or cloud synchronization service. Media is stored in private app storage; private notes use a separate AES-256-GCM encryption layer with a device-bound key. Your PIN and optional recovery key are stored only as salted, iterated verifiers.

Optional biometric unlock uses Android authentication. The app receives an authentication result, not fingerprint images or biometric templates. Optional face-down quick hide processes accelerometer readings locally; it does not retain or transmit motion readings.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display banner and dismissible interstitial advertisements. Advertising and consent services may process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information to provide and personalize or limit ads according to your consent choices, measure performance, and prevent fraud. We do not operate a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

Google may act as an independent data controller for some processing. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google advertising information. Where required by law (including the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and applicable US states), Google's consent tools present available privacy choices. You can reopen available choices at any time from Settings → Privacy & advertising → Advertising privacy choices. You can also manage or delete your advertising ID through Android's privacy settings and My Ad Center.

Import, permissions, and sharing

You choose files through Android's system picker. Import saves and verifies a private copy first; with your approval on supported Android versions, the app can ask Android to remove the public original from your gallery, or you can delete unwanted originals yourself. The app does not request broad photo-library access on modern Android versions. Older Android versions may request storage permission for an export you initiate. Internet access is used only by advertising and consent services. If you export or share a file, the destination app or storage provider handles that exported copy under its own rules.

Retention and deletion

Media moved to the recycle bin is retained according to your selected retention setting until automatic removal or permanent deletion. You can permanently delete items from the bin, delete notes, or use Settings to erase the active library. A decoy library is separate; remove its contents separately. Clearing Android app data or uninstalling removes local libraries, preferences, and the device-bound note key. Restore or export important files first: no cloud backup, account recovery, or remote PIN recovery service is provided. An optional local recovery key can reset your PIN only while app storage remains intact. Android cloud backup and device transfer of private app data are disabled.

SIYA Studio cannot retrieve or delete vault files remotely. Information Google holds is subject to Google's retention and deletion controls. Contact us if you need help understanding these controls.

Security

The app uses Android private storage, PIN verification, automatic locking, and screenshot protection in its normal release. These protections do not guarantee security on a compromised or rooted device. Keep your device, PIN, and recovery key secure.

Children

Calculator Lock is a general utility intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. Contact us with any privacy concern involving a child.
