Delete your data

No online account is created. Vault files stay on your device. Delete media from the library and then permanently delete it from the recycle bin. Delete notes inside Notes. Settings can erase the active library; any separate decoy library must be cleared separately. Clearing Android app data or uninstalling removes all local libraries and the note encryption key. Export anything important first. Import verifies a private copy first. Choose Move to vault and approve Android's request to remove the public gallery original. Declined requests, cloud backups, unsupported providers, and other copies may remain visible. Restore to gallery verifies a public copy before removing the private copy.

There is no cloud backup or remote recovery service. To reset a forgotten PIN locally, hold the calculator display and enter your saved recovery key, or use fingerprint authentication if you enabled it beforehand. Create or replace your private recovery key in Settings after confirming your primary PIN. We cannot retrieve vault files remotely. A recovery key cannot restore files after uninstalling or clearing storage. Advertising data held by Google follows Google's controls described in our Privacy Policy.
