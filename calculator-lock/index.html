Delete your data

No online account is created. Vault files stay on your device. Delete media from the library and then permanently delete it from the recycle bin. Delete notes inside Notes. Settings can erase the active library; any separate decoy library must be cleared separately. Clearing Android app data or uninstalling removes all local libraries and the note encryption key. Export anything important first. Imported copies leave gallery originals in place, so manage those originals separately.

There is no cloud backup or remote recovery service. We cannot recover a forgotten PIN or retrieve vault files. Advertising data held by Google follows Google's controls described in our Privacy Policy.
