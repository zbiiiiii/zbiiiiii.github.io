Paperlight Privacy Policy

For AR Drawing & Trace: Paperlight
Package: com.paperlight.trace
Effective date: 19 October 2026

SIYA Studio provides Paperlight, a camera tracing and drawing-reference app. This policy explains the app's current closed-test version and how information is handled.

Your photos and drawing practice

Paperlight uses the camera permission for its live paper-tracing view and for capturing your finished artwork. Camera frames are not uploaded to SIYA Studio. When you choose a photo through Android's photo picker, the app copies the chosen image to its private storage and processes it on your device. It does not request access to your entire photo library.

Imported references, saved tracing positions, favorites, rewarded-unlock status, practice progress and preferences are stored locally. The app has no user account, cloud photo processing, cloud backup or SIYA Studio analytics server. App backup is disabled in the Android manifest. Drawings saved to your device's Pictures folder and copies you choose to share are handled by Android and the destination apps you select.

Advertising and third-party processing

The initial closed-test build uses Google's official test advertising app and ad-unit IDs. No live publisher ad units are connected. Test ads do not mean the advertising SDK makes no network requests.

Paperlight includes Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen and Google's User Messaging Platform. The advertising SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app/ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the advertising ID or app-set ID. Google describes these categories as used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, with SDK data encrypted in transit. See Google's SDK data disclosure, Google's Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

Imported photos and camera images are not intentionally provided to the advertising SDK. Paperlight does not include additional advertising mediation adapters. Optional bonus references may offer a rewarded video; choosing not to watch leaves that bonus locked. Other drawing tools remain available.

The test build uses sample ads without a publisher consent message. Before live monetization, SIYA Studio will configure the relevant consent flow and update this policy as needed. Where the app shows an Ad privacy choices control, it opens Google's available privacy options. You may also manage or delete your advertising ID in Android's privacy settings.

Retention and deletion

Use the saved-drawing deletion control to remove a practice session and its imported reference from Paperlight. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove its private local data. Photos exported to Pictures, copies saved elsewhere, and files shared with another app remain until you delete them there. SIYA Studio does not operate a server-side account or photo database for this app.

Google manages the information processed by its services under its own policies. SIYA Studio cannot directly delete information held by Google. If you contact support, we receive the address and information you choose to send. We use it to answer and investigate the request, retain correspondence as needed for support or applicable obligations, and accept requests to delete correspondence we hold.

Audience

This version is intended for teen and adult drawing hobbyists, aged 13 and above. It is not designed as a service directed to children under 13. Simple animal or beginner reference drawings are practice subjects, not a claim that this version is a children's product. If you believe a child has sent personal information to SIYA Studio, contact us so we can investigate and remove correspondence where applicable.

Security and your choices

You can deny or revoke camera permission in Android settings and use Screen reference mode instead. Imported photos are optional. No Internet connection is needed for local drawing references or image processing; advertising requires a connection. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Policy updates and contact

We may update this page when app features, advertising configuration or data practices change. The effective date above will identify the latest revision.
