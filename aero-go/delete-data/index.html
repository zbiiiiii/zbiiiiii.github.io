Manage or delete your game data

Arrow Puzzle Escape: Aero Go (also known as Aero Go)
Android package: com.saadyaich.aerogo
Developer: SIYA Studio · Updated 18 October 2026

The game works without a SIYA Studio account. Eligible adult players can use a Google-managed Play Games profile to synchronize progress and achievements. Use the controls below to remove that game's Google data, local progress or correspondence sent to us.

Deletion can permanently remove progress and achievements. Close the game on your devices before starting. To prevent old progress from being uploaded again, disable automatic Play Games sign-in for this game and clear its local storage on devices that still have an old save before playing again.

1. Delete this game's Google Play Games data

You can do this on the web without reinstalling the game:

  1. Open your Google Play Games profile using the Google account you used in the game.
  2. Open Your Data, then Delete Play Games Account & Data.
  3. Under Delete individual game data, find Arrow Puzzle Escape: Aero Go or its earlier Aero Go name, and choose Delete.

This is the Google-provided way to remove data associated with this game in Play Games, such as saved progress and earned achievements. The game does not operate a separate SIYA Studio account database. You do not need to delete your whole Google account or Play Games profile to delete this game's data. Deleting the entire Play Games profile affects other games too.

Google's current deletion instructions · Manage this game's Google account access

2. Delete data stored on your phone or tablet

In Android Settings, open Apps → Aero Go → Storage & cache → Clear storage / Clear data. Menu names vary by device. This removes local progress, preferences and the local Play Games association for that installation. Clear storage on any other devices with old saves as well. Removing local data alone does not delete the Google Play Games copy; use the steps above for that.

3. Ask SIYA Studio to delete support correspondence

If you previously emailed us, you can request deletion of the information held in that correspondence:

Request deletion by email

Or email ibnyaichsaad@gmail.com with the subject Aero Go data deletion request. Tell us which correspondence to remove and, if possible, write from the address used originally. Do not send passwords, identity documents or payment information. We may ask for enough information to verify the request. We cannot delete your Google account on your behalf.

What is kept and for how long?

Local saves remain on your devices until you clear them. Play Games data is managed by Google until you use its deletion controls. SIYA Studio does not keep a separate server copy of game saves. Support correspondence is kept as needed to respond and follow up; you can request its deletion. Limited records may need to be retained for legal or security obligations.

Google may retain advertising, security, billing or service records under its own policies. Deleting this game's Play Games data does not automatically delete Google Play History or AdMob records. See Google My Activity, My Ad Center, and Google's retention policy for those controls and retention details.
